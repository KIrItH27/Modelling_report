\chapter*{Preface}

\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

Lithium-ion batteries are the fundamental energy storage engines
driving the modern transition to sustainable energy and electric
mobility. Extracting maximum power and longevity from these cells
without triggering catastrophic failure requires far more than
static factory calibration—it demands dynamic, real-time internal
awareness.

This report presents a comprehensive investigation into the
parameter estimation techniques that make this real-time awareness
possible within advanced Battery Management Systems (BMS). We bridge
the critical gap between theoretical electrochemistry and embedded
cyber-physical systems, navigating the mathematical landscape of
battery modeling to translate raw laboratory telemetry into
actionable algorithmic intelligence.

From foundational physics and equivalent circuit models to
next-generation machine learning and physics-informed neural
networks, this text provides a structured progression through the
methodologies required to track degrading parameters in real time.

We hope this work serves as a valuable and practical guide for
understanding the intersection of electrochemical systems, control
theory, and artificial intelligence.
