\chapter{Introduction}

\section{Overview of Lithium-Ion Battery Technology}

Lithium-ion batteries have become the dominant energy storage technology in
electric vehicles, consumer electronics, and renewable energy systems due to
their high energy density, long cycle life, and low self-discharge. Their
performance depends on accurate monitoring and control throughout their
operational lifetime.

\section{The Role of the Battery Management System (BMS)}

A Battery Management System (BMS) is responsible for monitoring voltage,
current, temperature, and state variables such as State of Charge (SOC) and
State of Health (SOH). It ensures safe operation while maximizing battery
performance and lifespan.

\section{The Need for Parameter Estimation}

Accurate parameter estimation is essential because battery characteristics
change continuously with aging, operating temperature, and load conditions.
Reliable parameter estimation improves the accuracy of battery models used in
advanced BMS algorithms.

\subsection{Safety, Aging, and Reliability}

Battery parameters vary over time because of degradation mechanisms such as
lithium plating, electrolyte decomposition, and electrode aging. Tracking these
changes enables the BMS to detect faults early and improve operational safety.

\subsection{Performance Optimization}

Accurate parameter estimation improves charging strategies, power prediction,
energy efficiency, and thermal management. These improvements directly enhance
battery performance in practical applications.
